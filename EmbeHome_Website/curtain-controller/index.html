EmbeHome Curtain Controller

Smart motorized curtain control for modern homes.

EmbeHome Curtain Controller is designed to control motorized curtains and blinds using EmbeHome smart automation. Although the hardware design may look similar to a 2 Module switchboard, the controller is programmed differently for curtain open, close, and stop operations.

Motorized curtain open and close control

App-based curtain control through EmbeHome

Voice assistant support through Google Home and Alexa where configured

Schedule-based curtain automation

Scene-based control such as Morning, Night, Movie, and Away modes

Clean wall-mounted switchboard-style design

Available in white and black finish

ARM Processor

128-bit AES Encryption

Control Options & Notes

Open curtain Supported
Close curtain Supported
Stop curtain movement If supported by installation
Automation support Use in scenes and schedules
Important customer note This product is not a regular 2M switchboard. It uses dedicated curtain controller programming for motorized curtain/blinds operation.
